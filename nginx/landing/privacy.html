Privacy

Which privacy policy applies to you depends on where you live.

  • If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our GDPR Privacy Policy applies.
  • Everywhere else, including Hong Kong, our PDPO Privacy Policy applies.

Both describe the same product and the same data practices; they differ in the legal framework they are written under. If you are unsure which one covers you, write to us at info@lovemuse.app.
