Hi Jess,

Muse works, it's on the phone, and the part that's missing isn't code any more —
it's the restaurants. Right now the app knows **11 places in Hong Kong**, so
"near me" comes back almost empty and the filters have nothing to filter. That's
the one thing I can't do from here: I don't know which places in Hong Kong are
worth recommending. You do.

**What I need:** your list of Hong Kong restaurants worth recommending. There's
no limit — send as many as you think belong there. Ten is better than none, and
two hundred is better than ten.

## How to send it

Fill in the attached file (Excel or CSV, whichever you prefer) and send it back.
The Excel file has a second tab with the tag list and an example row — the first
tab is the one to fill in, and it's empty on purpose. If you use the CSV instead,
replace the three example rows: they're there to show the shape, not to be kept.

You can also just write the list any way you like and I'll reformat it — don't
let the template stop you.

## The columns

| Column | Needed? | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| **name** | Yes | The restaurant's name, as people search for it. |
| **city** | Recommended | `Hong Kong` for almost everything. Only different if the place is somewhere else. |
| **district** | Recommended | The neighbourhood: `Central`, `Sheung Wan`, `Wan Chai`, `Causeway Bay`, `Tsim Sha Tsui`… This is what tells apart two branches of the same place, so it matters more than it looks. |
| **tags** | Optional, but this is the valuable part | How you'd describe the place, separated by commas. Only from the list below. |

You don't need the address, the phone number, the price or a photo — we pull all
of that from Google automatically. Name and neighbourhood are enough to find it.

## The tags

Pick the ones that fit — **three or four per place is plenty**, and leaving it
empty is fine if nothing quite fits. Please use these exact words: anything else
gets ignored, and I'd rather have a place with no tags than a wrong one.

**Vibe — what the place feels like**

`casual` · `classic` · `fine-dining` · `good-vibes` · `quiet` · `romantic` · `trendy`

**Occasion — what you'd go there for**

`brunch` · `business-lunch` · `date-night` · `drinks-bar` · `family-dinner` ·
`foodie-experience` · `girls-night` · `group-gathering` · `mums-babies` ·
`quick-bite` · `solo-dining` · `special-occasion`

**Scene — what the place has**

`family-friendly` · `instagrammable` · `live-music` · `outdoor-terrace` · `pet-friendly`

**Dietary — what it caters for**

`gluten-free` · `vegetarian`

If something you'd want to say isn't on the list, write it anyway in the tags
column and I'll add it — that list isn't finished, and the words people actually
use are better than the ones I guessed.

## What happens next

I load the file, the app finds each place on Google and fills in the address,
the photos, the opening hours and the map pin by itself. Your tags come along
with it, and that's what makes the filters work — "romantic places in Sheung
Wan with a terrace" only means something once places are described.
